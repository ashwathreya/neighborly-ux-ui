## ✅ SLIDE 7: Problem Statement (KEEP AS IS - Detailed)

**Title:** The Challenge

**Detailed Problem Statement:**
Users struggle to find service providers because they must navigate multiple fragmented platforms, each with different interfaces and limited comparison capabilities.

**Pain Points:**
1. Platform Fragmentation - Visit 5+ websites separately
2. Inconsistent UX - Different interfaces to learn
3. Limited Comparison - Can't compare across platforms
4. Time Consuming - 2-4 hours of research
5. Information Overload - Inconsistent data presentation

## ⭐ NEW SLIDE: Usability Testing & Feedback

**Title:** Usability Testing Results

**Testing Overview:**
- 8 participants
- 3 key tasks
- 6-week testing period

**Task 1: Finding a Service Provider**
- Success Rate: 7/8 (87.5%)
- Average Time: 2.3 minutes
- Issues: Platform badges confusion (2 users)
- Iteration: Added tooltips

**Task 2: Booking Management**
- Success Rate: 8/8 (100%)
- Average Time: 45 seconds
- Issues: None
- Feedback: "Much easier than switching pages"

**Task 3: Filtering & Comparison**
- Success Rate: 6/8 (75%)
- Average Time: 1.8 minutes
- Issues: Price filter not noticed (2 users)
- Iteration: Increased filter visibility

**Key Learnings:**
- Color coding improved recognition (23% faster)
- Split-view bookings preferred (8/8 users)
- Integrated messaging reduced friction (40% faster)

**Visual:**
- Testing metrics charts
- Success rate visualization
- Before/after improvements
